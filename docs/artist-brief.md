# Artist content brief — what is placeholding, and what to send

This page currently mixes finished craft with **unverified placeholder content**: the copy, training history, photographs, recordings and reels are stand-ins written or licensed for the build. Nothing about Chinmay's real biography, credentials, venues or repertoire has been confirmed by him yet.

Rules the build follows until this brief is answered:

- No invented fact is presented as biography. Lines that would otherwise lie are labelled on the page (`TRAINING DATES & CREDENTIALS · TO BE CONFIRMED`, `LIVE PERFORMANCE FOOTAGE TO FOLLOW`, licence lines under the reels and tracks).
- Audio and images in `public/` are openly licensed stand-ins, credited in the page copy.
- Creative copy that asserts nothing factual (headline, section titles) is treated as design copy and can simply be approved.

Each item below has a code (`C1`, `C2`, …). Reply with the code, the approved text or file, and any credit line you want shown. Items can be answered in any order.

## What happens when material arrives

- Raw files go in `Resources/`; optimised copies land in `public/assets/`, `public/audio/`, `public/video/`.
- Copy edits happen in `index.html`; booking addresses are set in the environment (`VITE_BOOKING_EMAIL` or `VITE_BOOKING_ENDPOINT`), never committed.
- Placeholder fine print is removed only when the matching claim is replaced.
- Each change is built, checked in a browser at desktop and mobile widths, and merged through a pull request.

Until then, the marked placeholders and licence lines stay visible — the page stays honest about what is not yet real.
